Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Video Portal is a private tool used only by its owner, Dirk Schembri, to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel. This page explains what Google data it uses and how.

YouTube API Services

Video Portal uses YouTube API Services. By using Video Portal, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Video Portal's own terms are in its terms of service.

Google data the tool accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • youtube: to confirm which channel is signed in, set video thumbnails and add videos to the channel's existing playlists.

The tool only acts on the owner's own channel. It does not access any other user's account or data.

How the data is used

  • Videos are always uploaded as private. The owner reviews and publishes them himself in YouTube Studio.
  • Google data is used only to carry out these uploads. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server operated by the tool, and the tool keeps no database of user data.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone. It is sent only to Google's own YouTube APIs to perform the uploads described above.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account, under Security → Third-party apps & services (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking it stops the tool from accessing the channel.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Video Portal's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email address shown on the Google sign-in screen for Video Portal.
