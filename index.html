Video Portal

Video Portal is a private tool used by Dirk Schembri to publish his own YouTube videos. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video, with its title, description, tags and thumbnail, to Dirk's own YouTube channel.
  • Every upload is set to private. Dirk reviews each video in YouTube Studio and decides himself whether to publish it.
  • Adds the video to one of the channel's existing playlists.

Google sign-in

The tool signs in with Google only to act on Dirk's own YouTube channel. It asks for permission to upload videos and to manage that channel's videos, thumbnails and playlists. It does not read, change or upload to anyone else's channel.
